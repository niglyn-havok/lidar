DUBLIN FLIGHT - DATA ATTRIBUTION AND LIMITATIONS

Debra F. Laefer, Saleh Abuwarda, Anh-Vu Vo, Linh Truong-Hong, Hamid Gharibi.

Changes for this game: projected local coordinates, footprint normalization,
LiDAR-derived coarse heights/roof estimates, terrain separation/interpolation,
closed building reconstruction, bridge approximation, source-image cropping,
resampling to 18.75 cm per output pixel, and material assignment.

Generic facade patterns are not measured facade photographs. Survey imagery
has incomplete coverage; the validity mask selects generic neutral material
where no usable photograph is available. Water elevation is an artistic
constant, not a measured tide. Excluded or uncertain structures and temporal
differences between 2015 survey data and 2026 OpenStreetMap are documented in
the accompanying provenance. This game is not a surveying or navigation product.
